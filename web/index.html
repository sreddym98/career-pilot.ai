Autopilot run

    Autopilot never sends anything for you. It finds and scores roles, tailors a cover letter and highlights for each, and holds them here. You read them, then Review & apply opens the Apply page — your tailored resume, cover letter and the employer's questions pre-answered from your profile — and from there the real posting, where you submit it yourself. Or approve a whole batch at once. Nothing is emailed to an employer, so there is no unattended sending to get an account flagged and no unchecked AI message going out under your name.

    What each run does

    ◆
    Scores fresh, open rolesRecent postings that still have a working link are scored against your profile; only those at or above your minimum fit move on. Roles that exclude your work authorization are never queued.
    ◈
    Tailors a cover letter for eachOne AI call per role, using only facts from your profile. Drafts with invented figures or leftover placeholders are rejected, not queued.
    ✓
    Queues it for your approvalRead the cover letter and highlights, then Review & apply opens the real posting with everything filled in, and you click Submit yourself. Nothing is emailed to an employer.
    ⊘
    Never over your daily capAll slots together respect your plan's limit — the total across every run can't exceed it.
    ⊘
    Never repeats a roleWon't queue a role you already applied to, skipped or have waiting — including a re-listing of the same job. Postings that close are dropped from your queue.
