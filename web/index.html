Autopilot

Runs on the schedule you set, prepares a tailored application for every fresh match, and holds each one ready — you approve with one tap, nothing goes out unreviewed.

One tap sends the whole batch — not fully unattended. Fully unattended sending from your own Gmail on a timer is what gets real accounts flagged as spam by Google's abuse detection, and an AI-written email nobody checked first is a mistake you can't take back. So Autopilot does everything up to that last step by itself, then hands you every prepared application in one list — approve all of them in one tap, or open any single one first if something looks off.
