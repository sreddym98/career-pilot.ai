CareerPilot ("we", "us") is operated by [LEGAL ENTITY NAME], Missouri, USA. CareerPilot is a job portal and career platform for QA and SDET professionals. This policy explains what we collect, why, who helps us process it, and the choices you have.

1. What we collect

  • Account data: email address, name, account type (job seeker or recruiter), and a password stored only as a bcrypt hash. We never store your password in readable form.
  • Profile and resume data you provide: headline, location, phone number, LinkedIn link, summary, work authorization, work history, skills, and any resume or cover letter text you enter or generate.
  • Job activity: jobs you save or apply to, application status, tailored resumes and cover letters, evaluations, mock-interview content, Autopilot settings and runs, connections you add, and support messages you send us.
  • Billing data: your plan, credit usage, and Stripe customer and subscription identifiers. Card numbers are entered on Stripe's pages and never reach our servers.
  • Integration data: if you connect Gmail, an encrypted refresh token. If you verify your phone, the verification status.
  • Usage data: timestamps such as account creation and last activity, and referral codes.

2. How we use it

  • To provide the service: your profile, tailored resumes, cover letters, job evaluations, mock interviews, Autopilot preparation and autofill.
  • To take payment, apply plan credits, and send transactional email (for example, notices that Autopilot has prepared applications for your review).
  • To verify your phone number, secure your account, and respond to support requests.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use it for advertising.

4. Gmail (Google user data)

If you choose to connect Gmail, we request only the gmail.send permission, plus your basic sign-in identity (openid, email). It is used solely to send application emails that you have approved. We do not read your inbox. Your refresh token is stored encrypted and you can revoke access at any time in your Google Account settings.

CareerPilot's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Autopilot prepares applications and never sends anything without your explicit approval.

5. Browser extension

The CareerPilot Autofill Chrome extension reads form-field labels on supported application sites (Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, iCIMS, SmartRecruiters) when you use it, and fills them from your CareerPilot profile. It fetches your profile from the CareerPilot API using a session token you paste into it. The token and API address are stored locally in your browser (chrome.storage.local). The extension never submits an application, does not collect browsing history, and does not send page content to us. Sensitive questions (salary, sponsorship, work authorization, demographics) are left for you to answer.

6. Cookies and local storage

We do not set advertising or analytics cookies. The website stores your session token and preferences in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in; clearing site data signs you out. Stripe and Google may set their own cookies on their pages.

We keep your data while your account is active.
