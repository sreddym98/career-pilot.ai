Illustration — your route from search to submit
01 — FIND

Jobs straight from the source.

We read the career boards companies publish themselves. Before a job reaches you, we request its apply link. Only links that answered are marked verified. Dead ones are removed.

02 — SCORE

A fit score you can explain.

Computed from your saved skills, title, experience and work mode. If a posting explicitly excludes your work authorization, it is blocked. With no profile yet, you see no percentage at all.

03 — PREPARE

Tailored, not templated.

Autopilot tailors your resume, aligns a cover letter and drafts answers for each role, then queues it for your review. Nothing is sent on your behalf.

04 — APPLY

You press submit.

Apply from inside careerpilot. The browser extension fills the employer's form and attaches your files. The final click is always yours.
